      How the numbers are made

      Pose is tracked in 3D at video rate (MediaPipe BlazePose, metric world landmarks, One-Euro filtered). Clubhead speed is estimated from your peak hand speed through the double-pendulum lever geometry of the swing. Ball speed, launch and spin come from your selected club's impact model anchored to published TrackMan tour data, and the flight is integrated with a spin-dependent drag + Magnus-lift model (RK4) calibrated to reproduce tour carries within ~2%. Form advice compares your tempo, X-Factor, posture and release timing against motion-capture norms from the golf-science literature. A camera is not a radar: treat distances as good estimates, not gospel.
